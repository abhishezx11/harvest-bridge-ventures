Ask us directly ↗
Who can work with Harvest Bridge Ventures?＋

We welcome inquiries from Indian manufacturers, suppliers and exporters, as well as overseas importers, distributors and businesses looking to source products from India.

Can you help us find buyers outside India?＋

We can discuss your product, target countries and buyer profile, then assess how we may support buyer discovery and introductions. Outcomes depend on product-market fit and the information available.

Can overseas companies source products through you?＋

Yes. Share the product specification, estimated quantity, destination and any quality or certification requirements so we can understand the sourcing brief.

Do you work on a commission or service-fee basis?＋

Commercial arrangements are discussed and agreed individually before work begins. The scope, responsibilities and applicable fees or commission should be documented between the parties.

What information should I include in my inquiry?＋

Please include your company name, product, quantity, destination or target market, required timeline and any relevant specifications. This helps us respond more usefully.
